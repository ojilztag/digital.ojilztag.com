Refund & Cancellation Policy

This policy applies to digital services and projects provided by OJILZTAG.COM. Project-specific written quotations or agreements may set out additional payment milestones and delivery conditions. If there is a conflict, the agreed project-specific terms will apply to the extent permitted by applicable law.

1. Advance payment

Unless a written quotation or agreement states otherwise, a 50% advance payment is required to confirm a project and schedule its commencement. The remaining payment schedule will be communicated and agreed with the client before the project proceeds.

2. Cancellation before work starts

If the client requests cancellation before OJILZTAG.COM has started work on the project, the 50% advance payment will be refunded. The client should send the cancellation request through the official communication channel used for the project so that the request and its timing can be recorded.

3. Cancellation after work starts

The client may request cancellation after work has started. However, payments already made, including the 50% advance, are non-refundable once project work has commenced. Work may involve time and resources already committed, as well as third-party purchases, licences, subscriptions, bookings or services that may be non-cancellable or non-refundable.

4. Third-party purchases and expenses

Domain registrations, hosting plans, software licences, API credits, subscriptions, advertising spend, messaging services, payment-provider charges and other third-party purchases are subject to the relevant provider's terms. Once purchased, activated, consumed or committed, these costs may not be recoverable. Any third-party costs specifically approved by the client remain payable as agreed, subject to applicable law and the written project terms.

5. How to request cancellation or a refund

Contact OJILZTAG.COM through the official WhatsApp contact link on the website. Include your name, project reference or description, payment details sufficient to identify the transaction, and whether you are requesting cancellation before or after work has started. Do not send card numbers, passwords, OTPs or payment PINs.

6. Refund processing

Where a refund is due under this policy, it will be initiated through the original payment method where reasonably possible, or through another mutually agreed method. Processing time may depend on the payment provider or bank. We will communicate if additional information is required to process an eligible refund.

7. Applicable law

This policy is subject to applicable laws in India. Nothing in this policy is intended to exclude or restrict any right or remedy that cannot lawfully be excluded or restricted.

8. Contact

For questions about this policy, use the official WhatsApp enquiry link on digital.ojilztag.com.
