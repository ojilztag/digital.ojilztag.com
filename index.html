Are the displayed service prices final?

Displayed prices are indicative starting rates for basic deliverables. The final price depends on the selected features, duration, complexity, revisions and any third-party costs. Small initial concept discussions are free; detailed creative development, design, editing, production and implementation are priced according to the agreed scope.

Integration may be possible depending on the service, API availability, permissions, quotas and security requirements.

How is the final price confirmed?

Displayed prices are indicative starting points only. We discuss your exact requirements, expected output, duration, complexity and budget, then mutually agree on the final scope and price before work begins. Initial idea discussions and small concept guidance are free; execution and production are chargeable.

Website hosting and cloud setup starts at ₹4,999. Event hosting with custom-domain connection starts at ₹1,999 when the customer purchases the domain. Domain registration, hosting renewals and third-party provider fees are separate unless expressly included in the agreed quotation.
