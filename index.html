Frequently asked questions

Good to know before you start.

Can I request a custom website?

Yes. Share the purpose, pages, features and any reference examples. We can discuss the scope and suitable implementation approach.

What is included in the MY EVENT package?

The combined MY EVENT package includes event website setup and hosting, event information pages, online registration forms, participant detail collection and event enquiries. It starts at ₹1,999. Custom domains, additional pages, advanced workflows and integrations are scoped separately before work begins.

What is included in the OJILZTAG.COM Classified Subscription?

The applicable subscription package, validity, listing inclusions and renewal conditions are confirmed in the relevant subscription terms.

Are the displayed service prices final?

Displayed prices are indicative starting rates for basic deliverables. The final price depends on the selected features, duration, complexity, revisions and any third-party costs. The exact scope and final amount are decided together through a discussion based on your requirements, expected output, complexity, duration and budget before work begins. No displayed starting price should be treated as a final quote. Small initial concept discussions are free; detailed creative development, design, editing, production and implementation are priced according to the agreed scope.

Can you create professional email accounts using my domain?

Yes. We can assist with domain-based mailbox creation, email provider setup, DNS records, authentication settings, aliases, forwarding, device configuration and migration. Mailbox subscriptions and domain charges are separate unless included in the quotation.

Do you build email automation and automatic replies?

Yes. Depending on the provider and requirements, workflows can send enquiry acknowledgements, follow-ups, booking or order updates, reminders and internal notifications. Bulk email and marketing campaigns must follow consent, provider policies and deliverability requirements.

Can you develop a CRM or custom management system?

Yes. We can plan lightweight CRM and management modules for businesses, schools, hospitals, clinics and other organizations. Features such as customer or patient records, student details, appointments, fees, payments, receipts, tasks and reports are selected according to the agreed scope, privacy and access-control requirements.

Can you integrate online payments into a website or system?

We can integrate supported payment gateways and plan checkout, payment status, order references, receipts and notification workflows. Gateway onboarding and approval, transaction charges, provider limitations and applicable taxes are separate considerations.

Can you build a small system within a limited budget?

Yes. We can start with a focused solution such as a digital form, spreadsheet-connected workflow, email automation, simple CRM or one management module. Features can be added in stages after the initial scope is agreed.

Can AI be connected to my website or business process?

Where technically suitable, we can integrate AI assistants, knowledge-based responses, enquiry classification and workflow support. Data permissions, privacy, API availability, accuracy checks and usage charges are assessed before implementation.

Can you connect Google services or APIs?

Integration may be possible depending on the service, API availability, permissions, quotas and security requirements.

Do you provide hosting and domain support?

Yes. Website hosting, domain connection, DNS, SSL and deployment support are available. Domain registration, hosting renewals and third-party provider fees are separate unless expressly included in the agreed quotation.

How do I get technical support?

Use the contact options on this page and include your project name, issue and relevant details. Do not send passwords or secret keys.
