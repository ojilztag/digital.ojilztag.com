Frequently asked questions

Answers to common project questions.

Can I request a custom website?

Yes. Share the purpose, pages, features and any reference examples. We can discuss the scope and suitable implementation approach.

Will my website work on mobile, tablet and desktop?

Responsive layouts are planned for common mobile, tablet and desktop screen sizes. Final behaviour can vary with browser versions, device settings, embedded webviews and third-party components, so device-specific testing may be included in the agreed scope.

How long will my project take to complete?

The estimated timeline depends on the approved scope, content and materials supplied, integrations, review cycles and third-party approvals. We confirm an estimated schedule and key milestones in the project quotation or brief.

Can I request changes or revisions?

Yes. The included review and revision rounds, what counts as a scope change and any additional charges are stated in the approved quotation. New features or substantial changes may require a revised estimate and timeline.

Who owns the completed website or project files?

Ownership, access credentials, source files, licences and handover items are defined in the written project agreement. Third-party themes, software, stock assets, fonts, APIs and subscriptions remain subject to their respective licence terms. Final handover follows the agreed payment and delivery conditions.

Do you provide ongoing maintenance and updates?

Maintenance, security updates, backups, content changes, monitoring and technical support can be discussed as a separate service or support plan. They are not automatically included unless listed in the approved quotation.

Will my business or customer data be kept secure?

We plan suitable access controls and data-handling safeguards according to the project requirements and available platform capabilities. Security depends on the selected providers, configuration and ongoing administration. Never share passwords, OTPs, payment PINs or secret keys through an enquiry form or chat.

Will you guarantee Google rankings, leads or sales?

No specific search ranking, lead volume, revenue or sales result can be guaranteed. We can implement agreed technical, content and visibility improvements, but outcomes depend on competition, market conditions, platform algorithms, audience behaviour and ongoing effort.
