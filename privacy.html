Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how the OJILZTAG.COM website at digital.ojilztag.com handles information when you browse the website or make a project enquiry.

1. Information you choose to provide

The project enquiry form asks for your name, mobile or WhatsApp number, optional email address, selected service and project description. The form is designed to prepare a message in WhatsApp. This website does not submit that message to an OJILZTAG.COM server by default. You review the prepared message and choose whether to press Send in WhatsApp.

Do not include passwords, one-time passcodes (OTPs), payment PINs, private API keys, financial account credentials or other sensitive information in an enquiry.

2. How enquiry information is used

If you choose to send an enquiry, the information in your message may be used to respond to your request, understand project requirements, prepare an estimate, communicate about the proposed work and provide related support.

3. WhatsApp and other third-party services

The enquiry flow uses WhatsApp links. When you open WhatsApp and send a message, WhatsApp/Meta processes information under its own terms and privacy policy. Your use of third-party websites, hosting providers, domain registrars, payment services or other tools is governed by those providers' own policies. OJILZTAG.COM does not control their independent data practices.

4. Website data, cookies and analytics

This website is a static information and enquiry website. The enquiry form is configured to open a pre-filled WhatsApp message; no analytics or advertising tracking service is intentionally configured in this website version. Your browser or third-party services may process technical data as part of normal web operation.

5. Sharing and disclosure

Information you send may be accessed by the people responsible for handling your enquiry and may be shared with a relevant service provider only where reasonably necessary to assess or deliver the requested project, comply with law, protect rights or address security concerns. We do not offer customer enquiry data for sale.

6. Security and retention

Please share only information needed for your enquiry. Messages sent through WhatsApp are subject to WhatsApp's systems and retention controls. OJILZTAG.COM does not operate a website-side enquiry database through the default form flow described here. If a separate server-side form or CRM is introduced later, its collection, access and retention practices should be explained before use.

7. Your choices and requests

You may choose not to submit an enquiry. For a question or privacy-related request about an enquiry you sent to OJILZTAG.COM, contact us through the official WhatsApp enquiry link on the home page and describe your request. Requests concerning data held by WhatsApp or another provider must be made to that provider.

8. Children

This website is intended for people seeking digital services. Do not submit personal information about a child unless you are authorized to do so and the information is necessary for the enquiry.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the website, enquiry process or applicable requirements change. The latest revision date appears at the top of this page.

10. Contact

For website and project enquiries, use the WhatsApp contact link provided on digital.ojilztag.com.
